About SentiMind

BERT+

What Makes SentiMind Different?

SentiMind is a context-aware sentiment analysis model that goes beyond traditional sentiment analysis by considering three key aspects:

Semantic Understanding

Analyzes the literal meaning of the words and phrases using advanced BERT embeddings.

Contextual Awareness

Considers the broader context in which language appears, recognizing that identical phrases can mean different things in different contexts.

Reaction Modeling

Models potential human reactions to the text, capturing the emotional impact beyond just the words themselves.

Technical Approach

SentiMind uses a sophisticated transformer-based architecture based on BERT with custom extensions:

  • Base Model: Fine-tuned BERT architecture for text understanding
  • Custom Layers: Specialized semantic, context, and reaction layers
  • Training: Cross-validation with regularization to prevent overfitting
  • Fine-Tuning: Trained on a combination of GoEmotions dataset and custom annotated data

Example: Context Changes Everything

Consider the phrase "I will kill you!" This could be:

Scenario 1: Between strangers

"I will kill you!"

Likely interpreted as a threat → Anger/Fear

Scenario 2: Between friends joking

"I will kill you, haha, just kidding my friend!"

Likely interpreted as playful → Joy/Amusement

SentiMind understands these nuances that traditional sentiment analysis models miss.
